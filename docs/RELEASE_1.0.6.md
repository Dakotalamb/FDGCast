# FDGCast 1.0.6

Recording Studio lets your saved recording look different from your live stream.

- Dedicated tab with three steps: choose a picture, adjust the layout, save and record.
- Clean gameplay, gameplay with camera, camera-only and whole-scene presets.
- Reuse existing OBS sources or draw a crop around part of the live picture. Move, resize, crop and mirror pictures in the preview; arrow keys provide fine adjustments.
- Follow the live scene by default, with saved layouts for individual scenes.
- One MKV recording by default. Advanced settings offer up to two extra files, size, bitrate, reduced frame rate, available H.264 encoders and up to six OBS audio tracks.
- Compatible active main-stream video encoding is shared for same-picture recordings. Different pictures require additional rendering/encoding. Existing webcam and game capture objects are reused.
- Explicit start/stop controls and optional start/stop with future streams. Recording errors stay separate from stream controls. An emergency Recording Studio stop button remains in the FDGCast Control dock when Companion disconnects.
- Unique filenames, local folder selection, available-space checks and clearer disconnected status.

Close OBS and Companion before installing. Saved settings are preserved. Recording Studio is free; paid overlay entitlements are unchanged. OBS's standard recording button remains separate.

See [Recording Studio setup](RECORDING-STUDIO.md) for resource costs, audio routing and supported behavior.
