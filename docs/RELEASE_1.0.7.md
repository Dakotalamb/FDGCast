# FDGCast 1.0.7

Stream Doctor is easier to read at a glance.

- Current status and a clear next step appear first, with up to three current problems.
- CPU, GPU and memory remain visible. Explanations and busiest-app readings are expandable.
- Audio settings, history, notifications and reports are grouped under More checks, history & reports.
- The Windows streaming profile is optional and collapsed until reviewed. Active or pending restoration stays visible.
- Disconnected, stale, missing and demo readings do not produce a healthy status. Old incidents remain in history rather than appearing as new problems.

This updates the Companion interface and presentation only. Existing monitoring thresholds, automatic restoration rules and OBS output controls are unchanged. Recording Studio and previous features are included. Paid overlay access is unchanged.

Close OBS and Companion before installing. Saved settings are preserved.
